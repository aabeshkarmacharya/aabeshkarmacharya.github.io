Three reasons the number above is not the price

These are asking prices. Nepal publishes no record of what land actually sells for. Every figure on this page comes from what sellers advertised on a marketplace, which sits above what plots clear at, by an amount nobody can measure from public data. Treat the estimate as a ceiling that the valley's sellers believe in, not a floor you can count on.

Your position is a tole, not a parcel. The marketplace records a locality name and no coordinates, so every listing in a tole is geocoded to the same point. Two plots a kilometre apart, one on the highway and one up a lane, are the same dot to this model. That is most of the 17% median error, and it is why the paid report exists.

Coverage follows the agents, not the land. South Lalitpur is thick with listings; the valley rim is thin. Where no listing sits within 3 km the surface is left blank rather than guessed at, and if your tole is one of those, this page will say so instead of inventing a number.
